# Evaluation examples

Cases in [EVALUATION-PROTOCOL.md](../EVALUATION-PROTOCOL.md) Section 5 are **fictional**. They show how to apply the judgment rules. They are not real investigations, customer results, or proof that the framework works.

Until a live pilot is completed and labeled as real, do not add examples here that could be read as measured outcomes.

When a real case is published:

- state that it is real;
- include the protocol version, sampling period, denominator, and conflict-of-interest note;
- keep raw evidence or a controlled review path;
- do not drop failed or insufficient-evidence records from the public set.
