# VGO Principles

**Reading copy:** https://www.vgoframework.org/docs/principles  
**Version:** [v1.0.0-draft](https://github.com/vgoframework/vgo-framework/releases/tag/v1.0.0-draft)


1. **Trusted visibility over raw exposure.** More mentions are not inherently better.
2. **Evidence before assertion.** Important claims should be supportable by first-party evidence or credible independent sources.
3. **Optimize for humans and machines.** Machine readability must not degrade user usefulness.
4. **Source diversity over manufactured consensus.** Independent corroboration is stronger than duplicated content.
5. **Measure longitudinally.** VGO is a growth discipline, not a one-time audit.
6. **Separate observation from causation.** A visibility change does not prove a specific optimization caused it.
7. **Integrity is a growth constraint.** Tactics that increase visibility while degrading truth, provenance or trust fail VGO.
8. **Open to new discovery surfaces.** SEO and GEO are components, not the boundary of the framework.
