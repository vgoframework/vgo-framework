# VGO Principles

**Version:** [v1.0.0-draft](https://github.com/vgoframework/vgo-framework/releases/tag/v1.0.0-draft)

1. **Human First.** Begin with real people and real needs, not platforms.
2. **Intent First.** Identify meaningful intent and problem spaces before selecting optimization targets.
3. **Effectiveness First.** Grow more Effective Visibility rather than maximizing raw exposure.
4. **Visibility is not value.** Ranking, mentions, citations, and traffic are intermediate signals, not automatic proof of value.
5. **Effective SEO over ranking alone.** Search visibility should help the right audience discover and understand an entity in valuable contexts.
6. **Effective AEO over extraction alone.** Answer visibility should provide accurate, complete, source-ready answers in valuable question contexts; extraction does not prove value.
7. **Effective GEO over mentions alone.** Generative visibility should support correct understanding, credible citation, meaningful comparison, and recommendation in relevant problem spaces.
8. **Separate domains, connected evidence.** SEO, AEO, and GEO overlap, but their observations and outcomes must not be collapsed into one ambiguous label.
9. **Owned assets and external signals reinforce each other.** First-party assets preserve knowledge and learning; credible third-party sources provide distribution, corroboration, authority, and diversity.
10. **Evidence before assertion.** Important claims should be supportable by first-party evidence or credible independent sources.
11. **Optimize for humans and machines.** Machine readability must not degrade usefulness to people.
12. **Measure longitudinally without assuming causation.** Observe change over time, but do not turn correlation into unsupported causal claims.
13. **Integrity constrains growth.** Visibility gained by degrading truth, provenance, or trust fails VGO.
14. **Assets can accumulate; visibility changes.** Organizations can retain content, knowledge, data, evidence, and learning even while rankings and AI outputs continue to change.
15. **Open to new surfaces, conservative with new concepts.** New terminology should solve demonstrated problems rather than expand the framework unnecessarily.
