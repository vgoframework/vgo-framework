# VGO Principles

**Reading copy:** https://www.vgoframework.org/docs/principles  
**Version:** [v1.0.0-draft](https://github.com/vgoframework/vgo-framework/releases/tag/v1.0.0-draft)

1. **Humans and intent before visibility.** Start with real people, real needs and decision intent before selecting queries, prompts, surfaces or optimization targets.
2. **Effective visibility over raw exposure.** Visibility is not inherently valuable; relevance to meaningful user needs and decisions matters more than raw mention volume.
3. **Trusted visibility over manufactured presence.** More mentions are not inherently better. Accuracy, provenance, authority and context constrain useful growth.
4. **Evidence before assertion.** Important claims should be supportable by first-party evidence or credible independent sources.
5. **Optimize for humans and machines.** Machine readability must not degrade user usefulness.
6. **Source diversity over manufactured consensus.** Independent corroboration is stronger than duplicated content.
7. **Owned assets and external signals reinforce each other.** First-party knowledge creates durable assets; credible third-party sources provide distribution, corroboration and source diversity.
8. **Measure longitudinally.** VGO is a growth discipline, not a one-time audit. Sustainable visibility should compound through stronger knowledge, authority, discoverability and measurement assets.
9. **Separate observation from causation.** A visibility change does not prove a specific optimization caused it.
10. **Integrity is a growth constraint.** Tactics that increase visibility while degrading truth, provenance or trust fail VGO.
11. **Open to new discovery surfaces.** SEO and GEO are components, not the boundary of the framework.
