# VGO Framework V1.0 — publication notes

**Status:** Published open methodology and action framework, in early commercial practice. **Publication date:** 2026-09-25.

## Scope of V1.0

VGO defines effective visibility growth across the machine-mediated world. It encompasses GEO's generative-AI visibility work, connects SEO and AEO, and provides a reviewable **Diagnose → Decide → Act → Verify → Learn** action cycle. The framework covers intent relevance, machine-readable owned assets, organization-controlled facts and evidence, credible independent corroboration, integrity risks, appropriate automation boundaries, and longitudinal learning.

Framework documentation and methodology are licensed under CC BY 4.0. Omseek initiated the framework and develops a separate product implementation. Implementations are responsible for making truthful claims about which software capabilities are actually available.

## Evidence and maturity

Use in an early commercial setting establishes practical adoption by an implementer, not independent validation, causal proof of revenue improvement, or general third-party endorsement. Do not publish customer names, private results or performance claims without a separately reviewed case and permission.

The [public evaluation protocol](../EVALUATION-PROTOCOL.md) remains **Draft 0.1**. It supports reviewable judgments for a single pre-stated user-intent scenario under its frozen V1.0 baseline. It does not yet validate cross-surface or longitudinal effective visibility growth, and it is not a certification. A separately versioned protocol is required for that scope.
