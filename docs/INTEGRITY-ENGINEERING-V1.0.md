# VGO Integrity & Poisoning Defense — Engineering Contract v1.0
**Status: FROZEN — 2026-10-01**

An anomaly is not automatically poisoning. Pipeline: Detect → Verify → Classify → Contain → Correct → Revalidate → Recover. Controls defend representation accuracy and MUST NOT suppress criticism or competitors.

Risk categories: entity_confusion, fact_conflict, stale_propagation, fabricated_evidence, provenance_concentration, coordinated_manipulation, compromised_first_party, prompt_injection_content, abnormal_visibility_shift, unknown. Disposition: signal|under_review|verified_risk|dismissed|recovered. Severity and confidence are separate.

Verified material risk may mark diagnosis uncertain/restricted, quarantine evidence, require human review, or pause Rank/enter Rank restricted flow. It MUST NOT apply an undocumented punitive score.

Every verified risk pins evidence, contrary evidence, provenance analysis, reviewer/reason, timestamps and policy version. Automated signals alone cannot label a third party malicious. Sealed questions, abuse heuristics and exact security thresholds stay protected while versions/hashes and public effects remain auditable. Payment/optimization status cannot suppress flags. Recovery requires fresh eligible evidence; history remains auditable.
