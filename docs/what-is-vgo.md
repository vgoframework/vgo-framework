# What is VGO?

VGO (Visibility Growth Optimization) is a cross-surface discipline for growing an entity's trusted visibility across conventional search, direct answers, generative AI, social discovery and emerging discovery interfaces.

The central shift is from optimizing for a single ranking system to managing **how an entity is discovered, understood, cited and represented across multiple systems over time**.

Machine-world visibility covers SEO, AEO, GEO, SMO (Social Media Optimization) and visibility in emerging channels. These overlapping capability lenses share fact, evidence, optimization and governance requirements; coverage does not imply a mandatory sequence, additive score or completed integration with every platform. SMO includes social discovery, sharing and accurate representation; exposure or engagement alone is not proof of effective visibility or business outcomes.
