# GEO 2.0: Generative AI Visibility within VGO

Method note within VGO V1.0; not a new system version or certification.

GEO 2.0 is our methodology and capability approach for GEO within the VGO. In generative AI contexts, it connects customer decision scenarios, verified brand facts and traceable evidence with diagnosis, prioritized action, execution, ongoing verification and maintenance. Its goal is to help brands be accurately understood, credibly cited, fairly compared and appropriately recommended in relevant contexts.

VGO is the overarching open methodology, covering SEO, AEO, GEO, SMO and emerging discovery surfaces such as agents. GEO 2.0 names the GEO approach within VGO; it does not denote the whole system or change VGO’s definition or hierarchy. Omseek is a product implementation based on VGO.

GEO is the general term for Generative Engine Optimization. Here, GEO 2.0 refers specifically to our approach within VGO, rather than an industry-wide version standard. Other GEO practices may also address facts, evidence and long-term outcomes. Effective GEO describes the intended outcome; GEO 2.0 describes the method for pursuing and maintaining it. The name is not a VGO version number.

## Five practice upgrades

These upgrades describe the requirements of our approach, not a claim that all other GEO practices only publish content or count mentions. Shared VGO capabilities support each upgrade; this chapter applies them only to generative AI scenarios.

### From mentions to accurate understanding, citations and relevant recommendations

Record mentions, factual accuracy, whether cited evidence supports the claim, and recommendation relevance separately. None alone proves selection or conversion.

Required evidence and output: Full answers, verified claims, citation excerpts and scenario-specific conclusions.

### From isolated prompts to customer decision scenarios

Define audience, need, market, language and decision stage; use question variants and repeated comparable sampling.

Required evidence and output: Versioned scenario and question sets, sample counts, failures and coverage limits.

### From publishing to brand facts and evidence assets

Connect approved facts to traceable evidence and maintainable owned assets. Publishing remains one possible action; independent corroboration remains valuable.

Required evidence and output: Fact and evidence inventory, source versions, owners and review conditions.

### From monitoring reports to prioritized action and ongoing verification

Connect each finding to a rationale, owner, authorized action, change record and comparable retest.

Required evidence and output: Prioritized plan, execution record, verification window and contrary evidence.

### From one-off delivery to ongoing perception and asset maintenance

Review stale facts, perception drift and evidence gaps; carry learning into the next cycle.

Required evidence and output: Review schedule, unresolved issues, asset history and next actions.

## Measurement and boundaries

Declare the scenario population, market, locale, surfaces/models, question and fact versions, sampling period, denominator and failure handling. Separate mention, citation, recommendation, selection, referral and conversion. Unsupported or failed observations are not negative results. Compare like-for-like samples; retain uncertainty and contrary evidence. Before/after changes alone do not establish causality. There is no mandatory GEO 2.0 total score.

## Fictional walkthrough

A procurement team asks whether a product supports local data residency. A generative answer claims a capability that the product no longer offers. Save the complete question, answer, citations, conditions and time; compare them with the current approved product facts. Prioritize the gap, assign an owner and update the relevant owned documentation. Request correction from external sources where appropriate. Repeat observations under comparable conditions, retain unsuccessful samples, and report whether the inaccurate claim persists. Record future review dates. This example is not a customer result or proof of uplift.

## Common questions

**Is GEO 2.0 the whole of VGO?** No. It names the GEO method within the higher-level VGO system.

**Does GEO 2.0 guarantee AI recommendations?** No. Recommendation is an intended outcome, subject to evidence, scenario and system behavior.

**Is Omseek required?** No. The open method can be implemented independently. Omseek is a separate product implementation; available features must be verified against its release and provider scope.

**Are SEO and AEO mandatory prior steps?** No. They often help, but the capability lenses overlap and do not form a universal sequence.
