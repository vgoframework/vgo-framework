# VGO Case Study Template

## Context
Entity, market, period, discovery surfaces and business objective.

## Baseline
Query/prompt set, engines/models, collection dates, search visibility, generative mentions/citations, authority and integrity indicators.

## Intervention
What changed, where, when and why. Separate SEO, GEO, entity, distribution, technical and integrity actions.

## Measurement
Use the same sampling protocol where possible. Record model/engine versions and uncertainty.

## Results
Report absolute and relative changes. Avoid claiming causation unless the design supports it.

## Integrity review
Were any inaccurate claims, suspicious sources or unintended effects introduced?

## Public evaluation protocol
If the case is published as a VGO public evaluation, pre-register the intent scenario and keep raw evidence as specified in [EVALUATION-PROTOCOL.md](EVALUATION-PROTOCOL.md). Report the denominator. Do not publish only the best screenshot.

## Lessons
What appears transferable, what remains uncertain, and what should be tested next.

## GEO 2.0 scenario record (when applicable)

- Customer decision scenario: audience, need, market, language and decision stage.
- Verified brand facts: supporting evidence, source version, owner and review date.
- Baseline: keep mention, accuracy, citation support and recommendation relevance separate.
- Priority and action: rationale, responsibility, authorization and exact change record.
- Verification: comparable sampling, denominator, failures, contrary evidence and outcome limits.
- Maintenance: remaining gaps, drift checks, asset history and the next review.

Use [GEO 2.0](docs/GEO-2.0.md) only for the generative scenario; preserve the broader VGO context.
