# VGO Framework Trademark & Brand Use Policy

## Purpose

The VGO Framework is published as an open methodology. Openness of the framework documentation does not mean that names, logos, marks, or other brand identifiers are granted for unrestricted brand use.

This policy is intended to keep third-party references accurate and to reduce confusion about endorsement, certification, affiliation, or official status.

## Names and brand identifiers

This policy applies to brand identifiers associated with the project, including:

- VGO
- VGO Framework
- Visibility Growth Optimization, when used as a project or source identifier
- official VGO Framework logos, symbols, badges, and visual identity assets
- Omseek names and brand assets

Use of descriptive terminology is distinguished from use that implies source, sponsorship, endorsement, certification, or affiliation.

## Documentation license is separate

Unless otherwise stated, framework documentation and methodology are made available under CC BY 4.0.

That copyright license does **not** by itself grant trademark rights or permission to present a third-party product, service, organization, certification, publication, event, or community as official, endorsed by, sponsored by, affiliated with, or operated by the VGO Framework or Omseek.

## Generally acceptable references

Subject to applicable law, factual and nominative references are generally acceptable when they are accurate and do not imply endorsement or official status. Examples include:

- "based on the VGO Framework"
- "implements concepts from the VGO Framework"
- "compatible with the VGO methodology"
- "an independent VGO community"
- citing or discussing the VGO Framework in research, journalism, education, or commentary

Where practical, link references to https://vgoframework.org or the canonical repository.

## Uses requiring permission

Please obtain written permission before using VGO Framework brand identifiers in a way that could reasonably imply official status, endorsement, certification, sponsorship, or affiliation, including:

- naming a commercial product or service as an official VGO product;
- issuing "VGO Certified", "Official VGO", or equivalent credentials or badges;
- operating an organization, event, publication, training program, or domain that presents itself as the official VGO Framework organization;
- using official logos or visual assets as the primary identity of a third-party product or organization;
- modifying an official logo and presenting the result as an official VGO mark.

## No false endorsement

Do not state or imply that the VGO Framework or Omseek has reviewed, certified, endorsed, sponsored, or partnered with a third party unless that relationship has been expressly authorized.

## Forks and derivative documentation

The CC BY 4.0 license may permit adaptation of licensed documentation subject to its terms. Derivative or forked works should be clearly identified as such and should not be presented as the canonical or official VGO Framework release.
