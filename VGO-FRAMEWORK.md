# VGO Framework 1.0

## 1. Purpose
VGO provides a common framework for organizations seeking durable visibility across search, generative AI and emerging discovery systems without sacrificing accuracy, provenance or trust.

## 2. Scope
A VGO program may cover websites, knowledge entities, product/service pages, editorial content, structured data, third-party media, citations, reviews, public documentation, answer-engine responses and agent-accessible resources.

## 3. Seven pillars
### 3.1 Search Visibility
Technical SEO, indexability, information architecture, query coverage, ranking, snippets and organic traffic.

### 3.2 Generative Visibility
Prompt/query coverage, answer inclusion, citation share, mention share, factual accuracy, contextual prominence and answer consistency.

### 3.3 Entity & Authority
Canonical identity, organization/person/product entities, source consistency, expertise evidence, first-party facts, reputable third-party corroboration and topical authority.

### 3.4 Content & Distribution
Useful source content, editorial coverage, digital PR, syndication with provenance, audience distribution and source diversity. VGO rejects deceptive mass publishing designed only to manipulate retrieval or generation.

### 3.5 Technical Accessibility
Crawlability, rendering, canonicalization, metadata, structured data, stable URLs, performance, machine-readable documents and access policies.

### 3.6 Measurement & Growth
Baseline measurement, visibility share, citation/mention share, query-set coverage, conversion outcomes, change attribution, experiments and longitudinal monitoring.

### 3.7 Visibility Integrity & Poisoning Defense
Source trust scoring, anomaly detection, factual consistency checks, impersonation/confusion monitoring, malicious-content detection, provenance tracking, correction workflows and recovery measurement.

## 4. Operating loop
**Discover → Diagnose → Prioritize → Optimize → Distribute → Verify → Defend → Measure → Learn**

The loop is continuous. A successful optimization is not complete until the resulting visibility is verified for accuracy and integrity.

## 5. Layers
- **Foundation:** technical accessibility + entity correctness.
- **Acquisition:** SEO + GEO + content/distribution.
- **Authority:** corroboration + citations + source quality.
- **Integrity:** poisoning defense + provenance + correction.
- **Growth:** measurement + experiments + business outcomes.

## 6. Principles
VGO should be evidence-led, source-aware, user-beneficial, measurable, reversible where possible, transparent about uncertainty and resistant to manipulative visibility tactics.

## 7. What VGO is not
VGO is not keyword stuffing, fake citation generation, mass low-quality publishing, fabricated consensus, cloaking, impersonation, prompt injection against third-party systems, or attempts to corrupt model/retrieval data.

## 8. Extensibility
Future VGO versions may add discovery surfaces such as autonomous agents, commerce assistants, vertical answer engines, multimodal search and machine-to-machine discovery without redefining the core objective: trusted visibility growth.
