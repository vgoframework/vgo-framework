# SEO, GEO and VGO

**Reading copy:** https://www.vgoframework.org/docs/seo-geo-vgo  
**Version:** [v1.0.0-draft](https://github.com/vgoframework/vgo-framework/releases/tag/v1.0.0-draft)

| Dimension | SEO | GEO | VGO |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary surface | Search engines | Generative/answer systems | All relevant discovery surfaces |
| Typical objective | Discoverability, ranking, clicks | Understanding, mentions, citations, answer inclusion | Effective and trusted visibility growth |
| Starting point | Search demand / queries | Generative questions / answer contexts | Real users, needs and decision intent |
| Core assets | Website/content | Sources/entities/content | First-party knowledge + website + entities + sources + distribution + integrity |
| Measurement | Rankings/traffic/conversions | Mention/citation/answer share | Intent-qualified cross-surface visibility, authority, integrity and outcomes |
| Defense | Search spam/security concerns | Source/retrieval manipulation concerns | Unified visibility integrity & poisoning defense |

## Relationship

SEO and GEO are major capability domains; VGO is the higher-level growth framework that connects them to real user demand, decision intent, authority, integrity, owned assets and measurable outcomes.

VGO does not collapse SEO and GEO into one technique. Each discovery surface has different mechanics. VGO supplies a shared strategic layer above them.

## GEO is important — raw exposure is incomplete

VGO does not argue that GEO is obsolete or unimportant. Generative systems are becoming important discovery and decision environments.

However, raw Prompt coverage, mention counts or answer share alone cannot establish business value. A large set of low-demand or low-relevance questions can produce high apparent visibility without meaningfully influencing real user decisions.

Therefore VGO distinguishes **raw visibility** from **effective visibility** and asks whether visibility occurs in problem spaces that matter to real users.

## Human-first / Intent-first

The VGO sequence begins before the query or prompt:

**Real users → real intent → high-value problem spaces → discovery surfaces → answers → brand understanding / mention / citation → comparison → recommendation → selection.**

This principle does not prescribe a universal scoring algorithm. Implementations may use different research, sampling and measurement methods.

## First-party assets and third-party sources

A technically sound, authoritative website can strengthen search discoverability and provide stable machine-readable first-party evidence. Generative systems can also rely on third-party sources and other data pipelines.

VGO therefore treats:

- **First-party assets** as the durable foundation for entity facts, products, services, knowledge, cases and owned content.
- **Third-party sources** as important channels for distribution, independent corroboration, citations, authority and source diversity.

SEO is an important foundation/channel, but not a universal prerequisite for GEO. VGO seeks reinforcement between owned assets and external signals rather than dependence on either one alone.
