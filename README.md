# VGO Framework

**VGO — Visibility Growth Optimization（可见度增长优化）**

VGO is an open methodology for systematically increasing an entity's **trusted visibility, discoverability, understanding, mention and citation** across search engines, generative AI systems, answer engines, agents and emerging discovery interfaces.

VGO 不是 SEO 或 GEO 的替代词，而是它们的上位框架。SEO、GEO、内容与实体权威、媒体分发、引用与提及、技术可访问性、监测归因，以及可见度完整性与投毒防御，共同构成 VGO。

## North Star

> Grow trusted visibility — not visibility at any cost.

VGO 的目标不是制造最多曝光，而是持续扩大**可信、准确、可验证、可归因**的可见度。

## Conceptual model

`Trusted Visibility Growth = Reach × Relevance × Authority × Integrity × Persistence`

这是方法论模型，不是固定算法。各行业可依据数据可得性定义自己的计算口径。

## Core pillars

1. Search Visibility — 搜索引擎中的发现、索引、排名与点击。
2. Generative Visibility — AI/答案引擎中的理解、提及、引用与推荐上下文。
3. Entity & Authority — 实体一致性、主题权威、第一方证据与可信引用。
4. Content & Distribution — 内容供给、媒体分发、数字公关与第三方提及。
5. Technical Accessibility — 抓取、渲染、结构化数据、机器可读性与性能。
6. Measurement & Growth — 基线、份额、趋势、增量、归因与实验。
7. Visibility Integrity & Poisoning Defense — 识别、预防、响应内容污染、错误实体信息、恶意操纵与不可信来源扩散。

## Relationship to SEO and GEO

SEO optimizes visibility in conventional search systems. GEO focuses on visibility in generative/answer systems. VGO coordinates both while extending the scope to authority, distribution, integrity, measurement and future discovery interfaces.

VGO does **not** assume that strong SEO is a strict prerequisite for every form of generative visibility. Search accessibility and authoritative web presence often contribute useful signals, while AI systems may also discover and synthesize information through other sources and pipelines.
