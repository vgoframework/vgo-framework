## Unreleased
- Corrected the framework taxonomy to recognize AEO as a distinct, overlapping capability domain alongside SEO and GEO.
- Expanded the VGO definition to cover search engines, answer engines, generative AI, intelligent agents, and emerging discovery surfaces.
- Defined Effective AEO, Answer Surface, Answer Unit, Answer Eligibility, Answer Presence, and Answer Share.
- Clarified that AEO is independently measurable and often supports GEO without making SEO or AEO universal prerequisites for every generative outcome.

## 1.0.0-draft — 2026-09-19
- Introduced VGO: Visibility Growth Optimization.
- Defined seven core pillars.
- Defined relationship among SEO, GEO and VGO.
- Added trusted-visibility measurement model.
- Added Visibility Integrity & Poisoning Defense as a first-class pillar.
- Added governance, terminology and contribution rules.
