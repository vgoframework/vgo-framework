# VGO Growth-First Principle — Frozen

Status: **FROZEN**  
Scope: VGO Framework definitions, standards, documentation, evaluation, ecosystem implementations and external communication.

## 1. Canonical priority

VGO = **机器世界可见度增长优化与治理**。

The semantic order is intentional:

**Visibility → Growth → Optimization → Governance**

VGO's primary objective is **growth of effective visibility in the machine world**. Governance is a supporting and protecting capability: it makes growth more correct, credible, durable, measurable and safe. Governance must never replace growth as the primary value proposition.

Core goal:

> **让机器世界发现你、理解你、相信你、推荐你。**

## 2. Global expression rule

All VGO definitions, documents, websites, APIs, products, training and commercial materials MUST preserve this priority:

1. **Growth first** — expand effective visibility in high-value markets, question spaces and machine surfaces.
2. **Optimization second** — identify gaps and improve foundations, content, search, AEO/GEO, evidence and distribution.
3. **Asset accumulation** — convert improvements into durable machine-readable truth, content and evidence assets.
4. **Governance** — maintain correctness, consistency, freshness, trust, integrity and risk control.
5. **Verification and learning** — measure outcomes, learn what works and feed the next growth cycle.

Canonical flywheel:

**Discover Opportunity → Optimize → Grow Effective Visibility → Accumulate Assets → Strengthen Trust → Earn More Selection/Recommendation → Verify → Discover New Opportunity**

Governance surrounds and protects the flywheel. **Effective visibility growth is the engine.**

## 3. Attack before defense

Customer-facing and product language SHOULD follow:

**Opportunity → Growth → Competition → Optimization → Evidence/Trust → Verification → Governance/Risk**

Not:

**Risk → Correction → Governance → Defense → Growth**

The first customer value is more high-value visibility and more opportunities to be discovered, understood, trusted and selected. Error correction, negative information, poisoning defense and risk controls are important, but secondary defensive capabilities.

## 4. Effective visibility

VGO does not optimize visibility for visibility's sake. Growth must target **effective visibility**: relevant, correct, credible, sufficiently stable, verifiable, actionable and sustainable.

Therefore:

- more mentions alone are not the goal;
- branded-query visibility alone is not the goal;
- content or media volume alone is not the goal;
- a single AI screenshot is not sufficient evidence of durable improvement.

## 5. Relationship to GEO

GEO remains an important component of VGO.

Traditional GEO primarily focuses on visibility in generative AI answers. VGO extends the optimization object to the wider machine world and extends the objective from visibility to **effective visibility growth**, supported by foundations, evidence, measurement, execution, verification and governance.

Canonical framing:

> **GEO helps improve visibility in generative AI. VGO grows and continuously optimizes effective visibility across the machine world.**

## 6. Language lint rule

Any VGO expression is considered semantically drifted if a reasonable reader finishes it believing VGO is primarily a governance, compliance, monitoring, correction or risk-management framework rather than a growth and optimization framework.

When drift is detected, rewrite in this order:

**growth outcome first → optimization mechanism → evidence/asset → verification → governance/risk**

## 7. Non-negotiable canonical statements

- **Definition:** 机器世界可见度增长优化与治理。
- **Primary objective:** 增长机器世界有效可见度。
- **Core goal:** 让机器世界发现你、理解你、相信你、推荐你。
- **Principle:** 增长在前，治理在后；机会在前，风险在后；进攻在前，防守在后。
- **Governance role:** 让增长更正确、更可信、更持续、更安全，而不是取代增长。
