# Contributing to VGO

**Reading copy:** https://www.vgoframework.org/docs/contributing  
**Version:** VGO V1.0 published methodology; v1.0.0-draft remains a historical snapshot.


Contributions are welcome through Issues and Pull Requests.

Good contributions include: clearer definitions, reproducible measurement methods, documented research, new discovery-surface considerations, integrity/defense improvements, case-study templates and corrections.

Please avoid promotional submissions, unverifiable claims, fabricated evidence, instructions for manipulating third-party systems, competitor attacks or mass-generated content without substantive contribution.

## Proposal format
1. Problem / ambiguity
2. Proposed change
3. Evidence or rationale
4. Expected impact
5. Risks / counterarguments
6. Suggested wording or metric

Maintainers retain final merge/release authority. Submission does not guarantee inclusion.
